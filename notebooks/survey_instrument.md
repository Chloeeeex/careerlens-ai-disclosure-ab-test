# Survey Instrument (Qualtrics)

## Welcome page

> Thank you for participating in this survey! We are CareerLens, an online platform connecting graduate students with career advice and professional guidance. We are currently collecting feedback on a sample article from our platform. Your responses will help us improve the quality of our content and services. This survey takes approximately 5 minutes.

## Part 1: Background

1. What is your current Heinz program?
2. What is your age range? *(18-22 / 23-26 / 27-30 / 31+)*
3. What is your gender? *(Man / Woman / Non-binary / Prefer not to say)*
4. How many years of full-time work experience do you have? *(0-1 / 1-3 / 3-5 / 5+)*
5. How often do you visit the Heinz Career Center? *(Never / Once a semester / Once a month / More than once a month)*
6. What is your favorite AI tool? *(ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini / Other)*

## Part 2: Article and attribution (randomized)

Participants were randomly assigned by Qualtrics to one of two blocks. Both blocks show the **same career advice text** ("Highlight your transferable skills", plus six career growth tips). Only the source line differs.

| Condition | Source label |
|---|---|
| Control (human) | *Source: 2026. Mastercard. https://careers.mastercard.com* (framed as written by a MasterCard Careers professional) |
| Treatment (AI) | *Generated by: https://claude.ai/* |

See `stimulus_control_human.jpg` and `stimulus_treatment_ai.jpg`.

## Part 3: Manipulation check

7. Who or what was the source of the advice you just read? *(open-ended)*

## Part 4: Main outcome questions

8. I believe the advice provided is accurate and reliable. *(0-10)*
9. The source of this advice appears to be knowledgeable about career development. *(0-10)*
10. How relevant and practical is this advice for your career? *(0-10)*
11. How likely are you to share this with a friend? *(0-10)*
12. Would you pay $5 for a 15-minute session with this source? *(Yes / No)*

## Design notes

- An earlier draft included a "personalization" item. It was dropped because both groups saw identical content, so the item could not vary with the treatment.
- All rating items were aligned to the same 0 to 10 scale for comparability.
